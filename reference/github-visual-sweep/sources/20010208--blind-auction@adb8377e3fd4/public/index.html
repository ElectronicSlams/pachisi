◆●▲ Blind Auction

How to play

  1. 8 rounds, 2 relics each. Relics are ◆ ruby, ● jade or ▲ sapphire, worth 1–6 points.
  2. Bid in secret. Split your coins between the two relics (0 is fine). All bids are revealed together.
  3. Highest bid wins and pays. Everyone else keeps their coins. A relic nobody bids on is lost.
  4. Ties go to the player with fewer relics. If they have the same number, a random draw decides.
  5. Income. Everyone starts with 20 coins and gains +2 after each round.
  6. Score = relic points + 4 per set (one of each color) + 1 per 3 coins left over.

You have 30 seconds per round (the match creator can choose 10–60). Miss the deadline and you pass; miss two in a row and the house bot plays for you until you come back.
